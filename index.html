The home

Room to spread out, minutes from town

A pre-inspected home on just over an acre, with water features out front, a big screened porch, a split-bedroom main level and a lower level with its own entrance.

  • Arrival

    A waterfall pond greets you at the entry, and you come in through a very large screened-in porch.

  • Living and dining

    The living room's picture window looks out on a second waterfall feature, which sets the backdrop for the dining area.

  • Custom kitchen

    The dining area opens to the kitchen: floor-to-ceiling pantry cabinets, an island, granite counters and stainless steel appliances.

  • Owner's suite

    A large suite with a double vanity, jetted tub, separate shower and a large walk-in closet that opens to a second, newer laundry area.

  • Split-bedroom plan

    Two more bedrooms and a full bath sit on the other side of the main level.

  • Lower level

    Set up for guests, with a separate entrance, mini kitchen, bedroom, full bath, living room, study and laundry.

  • Workshop and shed

    A large workshop and a separate shed are included.

  • Pre-inspected

    The home has been pre-inspected. Ask for details when you request a tour.
